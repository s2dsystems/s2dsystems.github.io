About Us

S2DSystems is one of the few IT companies that provide professional services in software development. As a privately owned company, S2DSystems provides IT consultancy, software design and development as well as professional services and hardware deployment and maintenance to the following verticals:

• Government (Local and Central)
• Financial Services
• Telecommunications
• Energy and Utilities
• Health Care
• Education.

S2DSystems is located in Islamabad, Pakistan. The company offers full-fledged services for software development and engineering empowering effective near-shore management to its clients. S2DSystems continues to operate with higher client expectancy and flexible solution offers. S2DSystems is an service provider to establish instant and successful communication channels and support off-shoring and near-shoring business models, thus keeping the deadlines on time and on track.
